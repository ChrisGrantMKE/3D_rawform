# Product Context — 3D_rawform

## What Is This?
3D_rawform is an open-source, subscription-free spatial sketching application. Users draw
with a stylus on transparent 2D canvases arranged in 3D space, navigate with multi-touch
gestures, and create cinematic flythrough animations between saved camera bookmarks.

## Who Is It For?
- Architects and designers who sketch spatial concepts
- Illustrators exploring 3D composition without learning CAD
- Students and educators in design, architecture, and art programs
- Anyone who wants Mental Canvas / Feather 3D capabilities without subscriptions

## Why Does It Exist?
Existing 3D sketching tools (Mental Canvas, Feather 3D) are locked behind subscriptions,
proprietary formats, or single-platform restrictions. 3D_rawform is:
- 100% free and open-source (MIT license)
- Local-first — all data stays on device, no cloud dependency
- Cross-platform — Surface Pro (PWA) and Android (Capacitor APK)
- Open formats — glTF/OBJ export from day one

## Competitive Positioning
| vs Mental Canvas | vs Feather 3D |
|---|---|
| Free (no subscription) | Cross-platform (not iPad-only) |
| Open-source | Open file formats |
| Surface Pro + Android | Surface Pro native + Android APK |
| Standard export formats | Full offline capability |
